This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Bubble Drop (Android package com.bubbledrop.app, the "App") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is published by Saman Games ("we", "us", "our").

By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The App creates a game account identifier so your progress, coins and items can be saved on our servers.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, display name and leaderboard scores are used for sign-in, cloud save and leaderboards.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, plus IP address and usage and diagnostic data.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Game account and progress (collected by us)

When you first open the App, it signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication (a Google service). This creates a random user ID that is not tied to your name or email. The following is stored with that ID in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Game progress: levels unlocked, stars, best scores and number of shots per level
  • In-game economy: coin balance and booster inventory (pickaxe, shovel, axe, TNT), in-game shop purchases made with coins, and daily gift claims
  • Records of rewarded ads you have watched, used to give you the reward exactly once
  • Technical records needed to run the service: request timestamps, one-time request codes and rate-limit counters used to prevent cheating and abuse

Purpose: app functionality (saving and restoring your game), account management, and fraud prevention and security.

2.2 Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the App receives from Google your Play Games player ID and display name / avatar. Your player ID is linked to your game account so your progress can be restored on another device. The App also:

  • submits your scores to Google Play Games leaderboards, where your display name, avatar and score are visible to other players, and
  • loads your Play Games friends list to show the Friends leaderboard, only if you allow this in the Google prompt.

Play Games data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not store your friends list on our servers.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and, where you have consented, personalize ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • Device or other identifiers: Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and similar identifiers
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country/region) may be derived
  • App interactions with ads (views, taps, ad completion)
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size

When you finish a rewarded ad, AdMob sends our server a verification message containing your game user ID and a transaction ID, so that we can grant the reward. Information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

2.4 Data from the Firebase SDKs

To provide authentication and database services, Google Firebase may process your IP address and device and app identifiers (such as a Firebase installation identifier) on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

2.5 Data stored only on your device

Settings such as music and sound volume, along with a local copy of your progress and any results waiting to be sent while you are offline, are stored on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this local data.

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, messages, calendar, health or financial information. The App does not offer real-money purchases.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the game: save and sync your progress, coins, items and daily rewards
  • To show leaderboards
  • To show ads and grant rewards for rewarded ads
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure
  • To fix problems and maintain the service

4. Sharing of information

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of the App for us, and only for the purposes above:

Provider Purpose Data
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) Account, cloud save, game economy User ID, game progress and economy data, IP address, app/device identifiers
Google Play Games Services Sign-in, leaderboards, friends Player ID, display name, avatar, scores, friends list (with permission)
Google AdMob Advertising, ad measurement, rewarded-ad verification Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, device information

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public. We do not sell personal data.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Game account data is kept while your account is in use. Technical records used to prevent duplicate rewards are kept only as long as needed for that purpose.
  • You can ask us to delete your game account and all data stored on our servers at any time through our contact page. Please include your Play Games display name if you are signed in. We delete the data within 30 days of verifying the request.
  • Data held by Google for Play Games and AdMob is managed under Google's own policies and settings.

6. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Play Games: signing in is optional, and you can manage friends-list access and your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict processing or withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access to game data is restricted by security rules so that each player can access only their own account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
